About RoastMyResume

AI-powered resume analysis that tells you the truth about your resume

Our Mission

We believe job seekers deserve honest feedback about their resumes. Too many qualified candidates get rejected because their resumes don't communicate their value effectively. Our AI-powered analysis provides instant, brutally honest feedback to help you create a resume that actually gets you interviews.

How Our AI Works

Advanced AI Analysis

Our system uses Google's AI APIs to analyze your resume against thousands of successful resume patterns. We examine formatting, content quality, keyword optimization, and overall presentation.

Instant Feedback

Within seconds, receive detailed feedback on what's working, what's not, and specific recommendations for improvement. No generic advice - personalized insights for your resume.

Data Security

Your resume is processed securely using encrypted connections. We never store your resume files permanently - they're analyzed in real-time and then discarded to protect your privacy.

Continuous Learning

Our AI continuously learns from hiring trends and successful resume patterns to provide increasingly accurate and helpful feedback.

Privacy & Security

Secure File Processing

All resume uploads are processed through encrypted connections and analyzed in secure, isolated environments.

No Permanent Storage

Your resume files are automatically deleted after analysis. We don't keep copies of your personal documents.

Anonymous Processing

Our AI analyzes content patterns without storing personal identifying information.

Built for Job Seekers

RoastMyResume was created by professionals who understand the frustration of sending out countless resumes without getting responses. We combined AI technology with hiring expertise to create a tool that actually helps people land interviews. Our goal is simple: help you communicate your value effectively so you can get the job you deserve.
