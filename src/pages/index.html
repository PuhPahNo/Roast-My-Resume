Burning Questions?

Plain answers about the review, its limits, and your data

A quiet inbox is a reason to investigate, not proof that the document is the only problem. Start with our six-question cooked-resume check, then review your targeting, evidence, and extracted text separately.
Uploads use HTTPS. The application stores the file temporarily, extracts its text, sends that text to Groq for analysis, and removes the temporary file after the request finishes. Provider retention and email handling are explained in our privacy policy.
We support PDF, DOCX, and UTF-8 TXT files up to 10MB in size. Your resume should contain selectable, readable text. Password-protected files cannot be processed. For best results, use a clean, well-formatted PDF resume with clear sections.
The model is useful for spotting vague writing, weak evidence, and structural problems in extracted text. It cannot see every visual detail, verify your claims, or reproduce a specific employer's hiring system. Read our methodology before treating any score as definitive.
You can review another version after editing. To protect the shared service, request and provider rate limits apply, so rapid repeated submissions may need to wait.
Processing time varies with document length and service demand. The interface shows progress while your file is parsed and the analysis runs; unusually busy periods can take longer.
The roast format makes vague criticism easier to remember, but each joke is paired with a concrete editing direction. We also publish the scoring limits and before-and-after examples instead of asking you to trust a mystery score.
